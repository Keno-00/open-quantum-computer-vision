openqcv
=======

openqcv encodes images into quantum circuits, exports them as OpenQASM, runs
them on a backend and decodes the measurement results back into images.
Installation and a first example are in the
`README <https://github.com/Keno-00/open-quantum-computer-vision#readme>`_.
